SKG Digital Solutions ("SKG", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website skgdigitalsolutions.com and our products (InstitutePulse, TrackPro and SwapEnergy), how we use it, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Who we are

SKG Digital Solutions is a software company with its head office at 103 Geeta Colony, Jalandhar, Punjab, India, and operations in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. You can contact us at info@skgdigitalsolutions.com.

2. Information we collect on this website

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, the product or service you are interested in, and your message when you fill in our contact form, email us, call us or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Technical information: basic information such as your browser type, device, pages visited and approximate location, collected through standard web server logs and, if enabled, website analytics.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry, arrange product demonstrations and send quotations you request.
  • To provide, support and improve our products and services.
  • To understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it.
  • To meet legal, accounting and security obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data in our products (including student data)

When schools, institutes and businesses use our products, they decide what personal data is entered into them, such as student details, classroom photos used for attendance in InstitutePulse, vehicle and driver data in TrackPro, or rider data in SwapEnergy. In these cases, the customer organisation is the Data Fiduciary, and SKG processes that data on its behalf, under its instructions and under our agreement with it.

  • We use customer data only to provide and support the service.
  • Access to customer data is limited to authorised people who need it to provide the service.
  • Schools using InstitutePulse are responsible for obtaining any consent required from parents or guardians for processing students' personal data, including photos, as required by law.
  • Details of how classroom photos are stored, how long they are kept and how they are deleted are set out in our agreement with each school.

5. Sharing your information

We share personal information only when necessary:

  • With trusted service providers who help us run our website and products (for example hosting, email and form-handling providers), under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, court order or a government authority.
  • With your consent, or on the instructions of the customer organisation that controls the data.

6. How we protect information

We use reasonable security safeguards to protect personal information, including secure sign-in, role-based access, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and regular security updates. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected parties of a personal data breach as required by law.

7. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and for our legitimate business records, and then delete it. Customer data in our products is kept and deleted as agreed with the customer organisation, or as required by law.

8. Cookies and analytics

Our website does not use advertising cookies. If we enable website analytics (such as Google Analytics), it may set cookies to measure visits and page usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Ask us to correct, complete, update or erase your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and, if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

If your data is held in one of our products on behalf of a school or business, please contact that organisation first. We will help them respond to your request.

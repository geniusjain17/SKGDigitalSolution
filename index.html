How it works

Capture. Upload. Process. Done.

The same simple routine for every class, every day.

  1. Step 1

    Capture

    At the start of the period, the teacher takes a single photo of the class on their phone.

  2. Step 2

    Upload

    The photo goes to InstitutePulse in a tap. No names to call out and no register to fill in.

  3. Step 3

    Process

    The software identifies the students in the photo and prepares the attendance list for the class.

  4. Step 4

    Report

    Attendance is recorded and shows up in the dashboard and in daily and monthly reports.

FAQ

Questions schools ask us

What does a school need to get started?

Teachers need a smartphone and an internet connection. Our team guides you through setting up your classes and student records, and shows teachers how to take and upload the class photo.

Which schools is InstitutePulse for?

Schools of any board, including CBSE, ICSE and PSEB schools. It can also be used by colleges and coaching institutes.

Does it work for large classes?

InstitutePulse is designed to work from one photo of the whole class. In your demo, we can show you how it works with class sizes and classrooms like yours.

What happens if a student is missed or not clearly visible?

Photo conditions such as lighting and seating can affect results. We'll show you exactly how missed or unclear students are handled during your demo.

Where are student photos stored, and who can see them?

Only authorised staff of your school can access your attendance records. Before you start, we'll share our storage, retention and deletion details with you in writing.

Is student data secure?

Access is protected by secure sign-in and role-based permissions, and data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Read our Privacy Policy, or ask us for more detail during your demo.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the number of students in your school. Contact us for a quote. Schools that join our early pilot get introductory terms.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. We're running pilots with a small group of schools. Book a free demo and ask us about joining.
